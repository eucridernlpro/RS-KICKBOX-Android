Delete your RS KICKBOX account

Account and associated-data deletion request

Request deletion in the app

Sign in to RS KICKBOX, open Settings & Privacy, and use the account-deletion request option. This is the preferred path because it associates the request with your authenticated account.

Request deletion from the web

If you cannot access the app, email the RS KICKBOX support address from the email address connected to your account. Include your name and state clearly that you want your RS KICKBOX account deleted.

Request account deletion

Support email: support@rskickbox.nl

What is deleted

After the request is verified and processed, RS KICKBOX is intended to delete the app account and personal data associated with that account, including profile data and user-linked app records where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Data that may need to be retained

Limited information may be retained when necessary for legitimate legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution obligations. If retention is required, it should be limited to the necessary data and period and not used for unrelated purposes.

Identity verification

To protect members from unauthorized deletion, RS KICKBOX may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before the request is completed. Do not send your password, Supabase keys, API keys or other secrets by email.
