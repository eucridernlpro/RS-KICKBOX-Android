RS KICKBOX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the RS KICKBOX Android app handles information when members, students, trainers, or administrators use the service.

Contact: support@rskickbox.nl

Information the app may process

Depending on the features you use, RS KICKBOX may process account and profile information (such as name, email address, role, membership plan and profile photo); class schedules, bookings, attendance and check-ins; training sessions, homework, progress, assessments, challenges, badges and Fight Camp records; private coach messages and member group messages; training media and technique-review uploads; notifications, events and private-lesson requests; documents, support requests and referral information; and membership, invoice or payment-status records managed by the club.

Device and local app data

The app stores settings needed for the experience on the device, including language, visual preferences, media-player state and some local fallback data. The app may access files, images, videos, microphone/speech recognition, or camera/QR functionality only when you choose a feature that requires it.

AI Technique Coach

When the AI Technique Coach is enabled and you explicitly request AI analysis, the app samples a small number of compressed frames from the technique video you selected. Those sampled frames, the selected technique name and the active app language are sent through an authenticated RS KICKBOX Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI for the requested technique review. The OpenAI API key is kept server-side and is not stored in the Android app. If AI analysis is unavailable, the app uses its local structured coaching preview instead.

Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate members, provide club and coaching features, synchronize data across authorized devices, manage access and memberships, enable communications, support training and progress features, operate the service securely, respond to support or privacy requests, and provide features that you actively request.

Sharing and service providers

RS KICKBOX uses service providers needed to operate the app, including Supabase for cloud authentication, database, storage and server functions. OpenAI is used only for the AI Technique Coach when that feature is invoked and configured. The current Android project does not include an advertising SDK. RS KICKBOX does not intentionally expose private member data to other members unless a feature is designed for member sharing, such as community or group features.

Security and access

Cloud access is authenticated and protected by server-side authorization and database policies. Trainer/admin capabilities are checked by backend authorization rather than only by visible app buttons. Sensitive server credentials such as service-role or AI provider keys are not stored in the Android client.

Retention

Data is retained for as long as needed to provide the RS KICKBOX service, meet legitimate club administration, security or legal requirements, and process account or data requests. Specific retention periods may vary by data category and applicable obligations. When data must be retained for a legitimate reason after an account-deletion request, that retention should be limited to the required purpose and period.

Your choices and rights

You can update certain profile/preferences in the app and can request export or deletion of your account data through the app's Settings & Privacy section. You can also use the public RS KICKBOX account deletion page or contact support@rskickbox.nl.

Account deletion

An account-deletion request is intended to remove the app account and associated user data, subject only to data that must lawfully be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, accounting or regulatory reasons. Temporary account deactivation is not treated as a completed deletion request.

Children

RS KICKBOX may be used in a sports-club setting that includes younger members. Club administrators are responsible for using appropriate enrollment and consent procedures for minors and guardians where required. The app should not be used to collect unnecessary personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when RS KICKBOX features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.
