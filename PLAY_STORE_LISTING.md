## Full description

RS KICKBOX is the private training and club app for RS KICKBOX members, students and trainers.

Train, learn, communicate and follow your development in one premium kickboxing environment.

### For students

- View and book club classes.
- Check in to training with secure QR attendance.
- Follow trainer-built sessions, workouts and home-training plans.
- Browse technique, Academy and training content.
- Track homework, assessments, progress, challenges, badges and Fight Camp.
- Send private messages to your coach.
- Join member groups and club community spaces.
- View club notifications, events, documents and promotions.
- Access private training media and the trainer book.
- Use the persistent RS Music player while moving through the app or when the app is in the background.
- Manage profile, privacy, data requests and account-deletion requests.
- Use the AI Technique Coach for optional technique-review support when enabled by the club.

### For trainers and administrators

- Manage students, access levels and membership plans.
- Create and manage classes, attendance and QR check-in.
- Build training sessions and homework.
- Publish training content, media, notifications and events.
- Track student progress, assessments, challenges and Fight Camps.
- Manage private lessons, coach notes, documents, support requests and referrals.
- Control app branding, themes, visual assets and splash/intro behavior.
- Manage promotions and the trainer book.
- Use cloud-backed operational settings and release-readiness tools.

### Private club access

RS KICKBOX is designed for authorized club users. Accounts and permissions are managed through the club’s secure enrollment and authentication process. Available features can depend on the user role, membership level and trainer-controlled access settings.

### AI Technique Coach

When enabled, a student can select a short kickboxing technique video for coaching support. The app can analyze a small set of sampled frames through the secure RS KICKBOX backend. AI feedback is coaching support only and does not replace a qualified trainer, medical professional or injury assessment.

### Privacy and security

RS KICKBOX uses authenticated cloud access for connected member features. Private server credentials are not stored in the Android app. Users can access privacy controls and request account/data deletion from inside the app.

## Play Console App access / reviewer notes

RS KICKBOX is a private membership app and most functionality requires authentication.

Before submission:
1. Create a dedicated Play review student account in Supabase.
2. Keep that review account active for the entire review period.
3. Give the reviewer the exact email/password only in the Play Console **App access** instructions.
4. Do not place reviewer credentials in GitHub, screenshots, store description or public documentation.
5. Give the review account enough feature access to inspect the Student Dashboard, classes, media, privacy settings, messaging and other relevant student functionality.
6. If trainer/admin functionality must also be reviewed, provide a separate dedicated reviewer trainer account and clearly label the two roles.
7. Explain that AI Technique Coach uses a secure backend and may fall back to structured coaching if the external AI service is unavailable.

Suggested reviewer instruction text:

> RS KICKBOX is a private club app. Please sign in with the dedicated review account supplied below. After login, the Student Dashboard provides access to classes, training tools, messages, media, privacy/account controls and other enabled features. The trainer controls are role-restricted. If a separate trainer review account is supplied, use it only for trainer/admin screens.
